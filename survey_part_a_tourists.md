# SafarSathi -- Survey: Part A
## For Tourists and Parents (15-20 people)

**Where to conduct:** Mandi ISBT (when they arrive) or IIT Mandi campus gate
**Time per person:** 8-10 minutes max
**Language:** Hindi preferred, English if needed

---

## Section 1: Background

1. Aap pehli baar Mandi aa rahe hain ya pehle bhi aaye hain? Kitni baar?
   *(Is this your first visit to Mandi, or have you been before? How many times?)*

2. Aap kahan se aaye hain?
   *(Where are you coming from -- Delhi, Chandigarh, Lucknow...?)*

3. Aap yahan kaise aaye -- bus, train, apni gaadi?
   *(How did you travel here -- bus, train, personal vehicle?)*

4. Aap yahan kitne din ke liye hain?
   *(How long are you staying?)*

5. Kya aapka plan hai kisi jagah side-trip ka -- Parashar Lake, Barot, Kullu, Manali?
   *(Do you plan to visit any nearby places -- Parashar, Barot, Manali?)*

---

## Section 2: Pre-Trip Information Behavior

6. Yaatra se pehle, kya aapne road conditions ya mausam check kiya?
   *(Before traveling, did you check road conditions or weather?)*
   - [YES] Kahan se check kiya? *(Where did you check?)*
   - [NO] Kyun nahin check kiya? *(Why not?)*

7. Aapne kisi se poochha kya ki rasta theek hai ya nahin? Kissse?
   *(Did you ask anyone whether the road was safe? Who?)*
   - Probe options: family/friends, taxi driver, online group, nobody

8. Kya aapko pata tha ki raaste mein internet nahin milega?
   *(Did you know there would be no internet on the route?)*
   - [YES] Toh aapne kya backup rakha? *(What backup did you arrange?)*

9. Kya aapko pata tha ki nearest hospital kitni door hai?
   *(Did you know how far the nearest hospital was before starting?)*

10. Altitude ke baare mein -- kya aapko pata tha ki Parashar ya Manali mein itni unchai hai?
    *(Did you know about the altitude at Parashar/Manali before going?)*
    - Kya aapko symptoms pata hain? Kya aapne koi taiyaari ki -- paani, dawai?
    *(Do you know the symptoms? Did you prepare -- water, medication?)*

---

## Section 3: Incidents and Near-Misses

11. Kya kabhi aisa hua ki aap raaste par the aur kuch hua jo aapko dar gaya -- raasta toota, mausam kharab, gaadi kharab?
    *(Has anything ever happened on a mountain road that scared you -- road washed out, bad weather, breakdown?)*
    - [YES] Kya hua? Aapne kya kiya?
    *(What happened? What did you do?)*

12. Kya aapne kabhi kisi raaste par jaana shuru kiya aur phir wapas aana pada, kyunki raasta theek nahin tha?
    *(Have you ever started on a route and had to turn back because conditions were bad?)*

13. Kya aapne kabhi kisi aur ko aisa hote dekha?
    *(Have you seen this happen to someone else?)*

14. Emergency mein aap kya karte -- 108 ambulance number aapko pata hai?
    *(In an emergency on a mountain road, what would you do? Do you know the 108 number?)*

---

## Section 4: Technology and Channel Preferences

15. Aap WhatsApp kitna use karte hain -- bahut zyada, thoda, ya bilkul nahin?
    *(How much do you use WhatsApp -- a lot, a little, or not at all?)*

16. Agar ek WhatsApp number hota jahan aap sirf "Parashar" type karo aur woh bata de ki raasta safe hai ya nahin -- kya aap use karte?
    *(If there was a WhatsApp number where you just type "Parashar" and it tells you if the road is safe -- would you use it?)*
    - Kyun / kyun nahin? *(Why / why not?)*

17. Kya aap phone mein naya app download karte hain?
    *(Do you download new apps on your phone?)*
    - [NO] Toh WhatsApp ya SMS zyada helpful lagega?
    *(Would WhatsApp or SMS be more helpful then?)*

18. Agar bus stand ya IIT Mandi gate par ek chota sa card hota jisme emergency numbers aur route safety liki hoti -- kya aap use apne paas rakhte?
    *(If there was a small card at the bus stand with emergency numbers and route safety info -- would you keep it with you?)*

19. Hindi mein voice message sunna pasand karenge ya padhna?
    *(Would you prefer to hear a Hindi voice message or read written information?)*

---

## Section 5: Trust and Usefulness

20. Agar koi WhatsApp bot aapko bata de ki "Parashar raasta KHATRA -- abhi mat jao" -- kya aap maanenge? Kyun?
    *(If a WhatsApp bot tells you "Parashar route HIGH RISK -- don't go now" -- would you trust it? Why?)*

21. Kya aap zyada trust karte -- ek local taxi driver ki baat, government ki website, ya koi app?
    *(Who would you trust more -- a local taxi driver, a government website, or an app?)*

22. Agar aapko yeh sab jaankari milti trip se pehle -- kya aap apna plan badal lete?
    *(If you had all this safety information before your trip -- would you change your plans based on it?)*

---

## Interviewer Notes

- **Sections 2 and 3 are the most important** -- let people talk, don't rush
- If someone mentions a real incident, slow down and get the full story
- Section 5 tells you whether the product concept will work -- watch for hesitation on question 20
- End with: "Kuch aur batana chahenge?" *(Anything else you want to share?)*
- Note their age range, travel mode, and comfort with smartphone in your records
