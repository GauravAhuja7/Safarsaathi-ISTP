# SafarSathi -- Survey: Part B
## For Local Stakeholders (10-15 people)

**Who to target:** Taxi drivers (Mandi ISBT, Parashar route, Barot route), dhaba owners (Baggi, Pandoh, Ghatasani), homestay owners near key routes
**Where to conduct:** At their workplace -- taxi stand, dhaba, homestay
**Time per person:** 15-20 minutes (more conversational, less structured)
**Language:** Hindi, local dialect if comfortable

> Start with chai. Build rapport before pulling out any notes. These interviews work best as conversations, not questionnaires. Memorize the flow -- don't read from this sheet in front of them.

---

## Section 1: Their Role in the Information Flow

1. Aap kitne saalon se yahan kaam kar rahe hain?
   *(How many years have you been working here?)*

2. Roz kitne tourists milte hain aapko?
   *(How many tourists do you typically see in a day?)*

3. Tourists aapsse sabse zyada kya poochhte hain?
   *(What do tourists ask you most often?)*
   - Probe: Road condition? Weather? Hospital? Petrol pump? Where to stay?

4. Kya aapke paas aise tourists aate hain jo bilkul nahin jaante ki raasta kaisa hai -- aur aap unhe rokna chahte hain?
   *(Do tourists come to you with no idea what the road is like -- and you want to stop them from going?)*
   - [YES] Kya woh maante hain? Ya phir bhi chale jaate hain?
   *(Do they listen? Or do they go anyway?)*

5. Monsoon mein sabse zyada kya problem hoti hai tourists ko?
   *(In monsoon, what is the biggest problem tourists face?)*

---

## Section 2: Current Information Sharing Behavior

6. Jab aapko pata chalta hai ki raasta band hai ya kharab hai -- aap kya karte hain? Kisi ko batate hain?
   *(When you know a road is blocked or dangerous -- what do you do? Do you tell anyone?)*
   - Do they call each other? WhatsApp groups among drivers?

7. Kya aapka koi WhatsApp group hai -- taxi drivers ka, ya is raaste par logo ka?
   *(Do you have any WhatsApp group -- taxi drivers or people on this route?)*
   - Usme kya share karte hain log? Kitna active hai?
   *(What do people share there? How active is it?)*

8. Koi official jagah hai kahan aap road band hone ki khabar dete hain -- PWD, police?
   *(Is there any official place you report road closures to -- PWD, police?)*

9. Agar aapko pata chale ki Baggi ke aage raasta kharab hai -- aap kisi tourist ko kaise batate hain jo abhi Mandi mein hai?
   *(If you know the road past Baggi is bad -- how would you warn a tourist who is still in Mandi?)*

---

## Section 3: Willingness to Participate

10. Agar ek WhatsApp number hota jahan aap sirf "raasta theek hai" ya "raasta band hai" type karke bhejo -- aur iske badle mein aapko tourists direct refer kiye jaayein -- kya aap yeh karenge?
    *(If there was a WhatsApp number where you could send road conditions -- and in return tourists would be referred to you -- would you do it?)*

11. Aapke liye sabse zyada useful kya hoga -- tourist referrals, ya kuch aur?
    *(What would be most useful to you -- tourist referrals, or something else?)*

12. Ek chhota sa message karne mein kitna time lagega? Kya yeh kaam mein badha nahin aayega?
    *(How much time would sending a short message take? Would it get in the way of your work?)*

13. Aap WhatsApp par photo bhejte ho? Voice note bhejte ho?
    *(Do you send photos on WhatsApp? Voice notes?)*

14. Aap type karte ho Hindi mein? English mein? Ya sirf voice note prefer karte ho?
    *(Do you type in Hindi? English? Or do you prefer voice notes only?)*

---

## Section 4: Ground Truth -- Route Conditions

> This section is where the real knowledge lives. Let them talk freely. Ask "aur kuch?" (anything else?) after every answer.

15. Is raaste ka sabse kharab time kab hota hai? Kya specifically hota hai?
    *(When is this route most dangerous? What specifically happens -- landslide, flooding, mud, fog?)*

16. Pichle saal monsoon mein kya hua? Koi specific incident yaad hai?
    *(What happened last monsoon specifically? Any incident you remember?)*

17. Koi aisa incident hua pichle 2-3 saal mein jo aapko yaad ho -- tourist ke saath kuch bura?
    *(Any incident in the last 2-3 years you remember -- something bad that happened to a tourist?)*

18. Nearest hospital kahan hai aapke yahan se? Emergency mein kahan jaate hain log?
    *(Where is the nearest hospital from here? Where do people go in an emergency?)*

19. Petrol pump kahan tak milta hai is raaste par? Koi jagah hai jahan petrol bilkul nahin milta?
    *(Where is the last petrol pump on this route? Any stretch with no fuel available?)*

20. Kya aapko pata hai ki tourists ko altitude sickness ho sakti hai yahan? Kya aap unhe batate ho?
    *(Do you know tourists can get altitude sickness here? Do you warn them?)*

21. Agar koi tourist raaste mein faas jaaye aur aapko call kare -- aap unhe kya bolenge? Kahan jaayein?
    *(If a tourist gets stranded and calls you -- what would you tell them to do?)*

---

## Interviewer Notes

- **Section 4 is the most valuable** -- this is where you build the route safety knowledge base. Don't rush it.
- If they mention a specific incident, slow down completely and get every detail -- who, when, what happened, how it was resolved
- Watch for mentions of informal WhatsApp groups -- these are existing infrastructure you can plug into
- Question 10 is a direct ask for participation -- note their body language, not just their words
- End with: "Aap jaante ho koi aur driver ya dhaba wale jo hame milda chahiye?" *(Do you know any other driver or dhaba owner we should meet?)* -- this is how you find the next 5 stakeholders
- Record: name, phone number, location on route, years of experience, WhatsApp comfort level (text / voice / photo)
